Get Order Updates via Text

NexR customers can receive order status and fulfillment updates directly by SMS.

Text START to +1 (646) 921-7272 to opt in to receive order and fulfillment updates from NexR.

Once opted in, you can text questions like "What's the status of my order?" or "How many orders shipped last month?" and receive an automated reply based on your account's data.

Msg & data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies. Reply STOP to unsubscribe, HELP for help.
