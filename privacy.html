Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Fulfillment SMS Assistant ("the Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with it via SMS text message.

Information We Collect

When you text the Service, we receive your phone number and the content of your message in order to respond to your inquiry (e.g., order status, fulfillment metrics).

How We Use Your Information

Your phone number and message content are used solely to identify your account and generate a relevant reply. We do not use this information for marketing purposes.

Mobile Information Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is used only to provide the requested service and will not be shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes under any circumstances.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your usage of the Service (i.e., how often you text in with a question). There is no fixed number of messages sent per period.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of messages at any time, or HELP for assistance.

Data Retention

Message content and phone numbers are retained only as long as necessary to provide the Service and are not used for any purpose beyond responding to inquiries.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the account administrator.
