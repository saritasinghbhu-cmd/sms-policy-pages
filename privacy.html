NexR Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how I collect, use, and protect information when you interact with my SMS service, operated under the name NexR.

Information I Collect

When you text me, I receive your phone number and the content of your message in order to respond to your question about your order.

How I Use Your Information

Your phone number and message content are used solely to identify you as my customer and send you a relevant reply. I do not use this information for marketing purposes.

Mobile Information Sharing

I do not share, sell, or rent mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is used only to respond to your requests and will not be shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes under any circumstances.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on how often you text me with a question. There is no fixed number of messages sent per period.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of messages at any time, or HELP for assistance.

Data Retention

Message content and phone numbers are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your requests and are not used for any other purpose.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to me directly by texting HELP.
